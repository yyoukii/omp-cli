# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want omp-cli to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, issues, and pull requests.
- Welcome newcomers and questions, especially from people new to open.mp or PAWN development.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private report by contacting the maintainers directly. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
